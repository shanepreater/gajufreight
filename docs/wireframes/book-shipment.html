How the arbiter panel works

If anyone disputes the shipment, these independent arbiters decide how the unpaid amount is split. They can't be you, Tasman or the consignee.

The split applies as soon as enough of them agree. If they don't agree in time, the fallback share applies, so a stuck panel can never freeze the money.

The challenge window

If an attestor confirms delivery without the consignee's delivery code, the final payment waits this long so you or the consignee can dispute it. With the code, it's paid at once (ADR 0006).

These come from the accepted quote and are read from it on-chain when the shipment is created, so they can't be edited here (a mismatch is NOT_AGREED). They're public on the Gajumaru chain.
