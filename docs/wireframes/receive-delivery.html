What happens when you report

The final payment for this shipment is frozen, and an independent panel of 3 arbiters looks at the scans, photos and what you send.

When 2 of them agree, the frozen amount is split as they decide. If they don't agree within 3 days, the split agreed at booking applies. Payments already made along the way stay paid.
