When to fund a leg

Each leg is its own agreement and escrow between you and that carrier, paid from your own funds. The shipper's milestones pay you as the goods move.

Funding each leg just before it starts keeps less of your money locked. A carrier won't start an unfunded leg, so fund it before its pickup date. Shipper milestones that line up with your handovers (here, Rotterdam) bring money in when you pay out.

Cash flow

Most you're out of pocket: 1,515 木, from 1 to 28 Oct. You end 1,070 木 ahead. "In" is after GajuFreight's 1% fee; leg funding includes each leg's refundable fee bond.

The leg fee bond

Legs pay their carriers in full and carry no fee. When you fund a leg, you add 1% of its price as a bond.

Once this shipment ends, you settle the bond: you get back the same share of it as this shipment paid you. Delivered in full, you get it all back. If the shipment is refunded, the bond becomes GajuFreight's fee. This stops legs being used to avoid the fee.
