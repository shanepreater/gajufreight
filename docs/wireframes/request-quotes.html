Dispute terms you set these; every quote carries them unchanged

If anyone disputes the shipment, these arbiters decide how the unpaid amount is split. Forwarders set the price, schedule, deadline and attestors; you choose who settles disputes over your money.

How the arbiter panel works

The split applies as soon as enough arbiters agree. If they can't agree within the decision window, the fallback share goes to the forwarder and the rest comes back to you, so a stuck panel can never freeze the money.

Forwarders see your panel before quoting. One that objects can decline, so pick arbiters both sides can trust.

The challenge window

If an attestor confirms delivery without the consignee's delivery code, the final payment waits this long so you or the consignee can dispute it. With the code, it's paid at once (ADR 0006).

Defaults: 2 of 3 arbiters within 3 days, else 50%, and a 1-day challenge window. These terms, including the arbiters' accounts, are public on-chain as soon as you send the request.
