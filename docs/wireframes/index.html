GajuFreight wireframes

Clickable low-fidelity screens for every party. They follow the user journeys in docs/ux/ and use the demo's fictional parties and route (shipment GF-2026-0008: three pallets, Yantian → Rotterdam → Tilburg).

Dashed boxes like this are reviewer notes explaining a design choice. They're not part of the product.
