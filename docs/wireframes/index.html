GajuFreight wireframes

Clickable screens for every party, with a low-fi layout in the GajuFreight brand. They follow the user journeys in docs/ux/ and use the demo's fictional parties and route (shipment GF-2026-0008: three pallets, Yantian → Rotterdam → Tilburg).

Two ways to review the same screens

Locally: open this file (docs/wireframes/index.html) in any browser, or run npm run open --prefix scripts/wireframes. No server or install is needed.

Online: the private claude.ai review page shows the same screens and prompts, works on a phone, and takes comments. It's republished each round.

How to review

  1. Start with your own role below and follow the links as that person would.
  2. Ask whether each person can finish their job without help, and whether anything surprises them.
  3. Comment on the review page, or in the PR. The next round lands in both places.

New in round 5: your round 4 review

Each change answers a point in the round 4 feedback. The design behind it is in ADRs 0006 to 0009.

  • Every screen: a Feedback button in the header, a footer with the version and a support reference, and ? pop-ups for the detail (items 3, 6, 7).
  • Missing items: the "1 missing" badge leads to who had it last and who should have it now (items 1, 2).
  • Labels a handler can check by eye; leg cash flow by date (items 4, 5).
  • Sign in once per shift and switch handlers on a shared phone (item 8).
  • A searchable forwarder directory, and company sign-up with admin verification (items 10, 14).
  • The driver proves delivery, and the consignee gets simple tracking (items 11, 12). Bulk shipping (item 13) waits for a spike.

Decisions taken in round 2

  1. Booking and funding: one atomic signature (ADR 0005).
  2. Missing package at a scan: confirm in the app with a reason; not blocking.
  3. Panel votes: hidden from an arbiter until they've voted (privacy standard).
  4. Wrong-stage errors: "That isn't possible at this stage of the shipment."
  5. Negotiation rounds: limited to N, set by the Admin team (initially 5).
  6. Margins and leg costs: private to the forwarder and that carrier in the app.

Round 3 applies them: the Admin settings screen, "Round 3 of 5" and the final offer in negotiation, the missing-package confirmation in the scan, and privacy notes on legs.
