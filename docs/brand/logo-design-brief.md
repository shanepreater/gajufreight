# Logo Design Brief: GajuFreight

## 1. Project Overview & Background
* **Brand Name:** GajuFreight
* **Parent Ecosystem:** Gajumaru / QPQ Ecosystem (Gaju Pay, GajuMarket, GajuMobile, GajuDesk, GajuDEX)
* **Core Purpose:** Freight, shipping, and logistics infrastructure powering the Gajumaru ecosystem.
* **Design Requirement:** Deliver a modern vector logo in scalable **SVG format** that adheres to the established visual identity and geometric brand family of Gaju products (specifically mirroring the aesthetic of **GajuMarket**).

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Brand Continuity
The Gaju ecosystem uses a clean, tech-forward, minimal aesthetic centered around structural balance, clarity, and Japanese functional design principles.

### Key Ecosystem Design Motifs:
1. **The Symbol / Currency Link:** The core Gaju symbol originates from the Japanese Kanji **木** (*Ki*, meaning tree), reflecting the underlying Banyan tree root/branch architecture (*Groot* root layer with branching platforms).
2. **GajuMarket Visual Aesthetic:**
   * High-contrast, clean typography paired with geometric iconography.
   * Monoline or precise medium-weight geometric shapes.
   * Functional simplicity suited for web, mobile apps, and UI headers.
3. **Logistics & Freight Visual Integration:**
   * Needs to communicate **movement, transit, cargo, directional flow, speed, and global connectivity** without breaking the clean geometric language of the Gaju suite.

---

## 3. Logo Concept Options & Structure
The logo must consist of an **Icon (Mark)** + **Logotype (Wordmark)** designed to work together or as standalone elements.

### Concept Direction A: The "Gaju Flow & Freight" (Recommended)
* **Iconography:** A stylized integration of the **木** (Ki / tree branch) motif crossed with forward motion arrows, container grid lines, or a dynamic isometric shipping box/cube.
* **Symbolic Meaning:** Represents trade moving along the Gajumaru network branches—seamless logistics sitting on top of the root resource layer.

### Concept Direction B: The "Geometric Intercept / Route"
* **Iconography:** Clean geometric lines forming a stylized **'G'** integrated with a shipping arrow, transit vector, or interconnected nodes/cargo mark.
* **Symbolic Meaning:** Represents direct point-to-point shipping, fast settlement, and transparent logistics.

---

## 4. Typography & Color Palette

### Typography:
* **Primary Text:** "GajuFreight"
* **Style:** Modern, bold geometric sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Montserrat, or custom geometric typeface matching GajuMarket/QPQ brand guidelines).
* **Treatment:** Clean kerning; optional visual distinction between **"Gaju"** (medium/regular weight) and **"Freight"** (bold or stylized weight) to preserve product family naming hierarchy.

### Color Palette:
* **Primary (Gaju Core):** Dark Charcoal / Off-Black (`#111827` or `#1A1A1A`) and Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Accent (Freight / Logistics Identity):** Industrial / Freight Accent Color (e.g., International Signal Orange `#FF5722` or Cargo Blue `#0066FF`) to distinguish the freight product line while harmonizing with the dark/light neutral base.

---

## 5. Technical Requirements & Deliverables

### Format Requirements:
1. **Primary Format:** Pure, clean **SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics)**.
   * Outlined text/fonts (converted to vector paths).
   * Clean node points and organized layer hierarchy.
   * No embedded raster images or unexpanded strokes/effects.
2. **Secondary Formats (Derived from SVG):**
   * High-resolution PNG (Transparent background, Light & Dark mode variants).
   * EPS / PDF vector master files.

### Required Variations:
* **Full Horizontal Logo:** Icon + "GajuFreight" logotype.
* **Stacked Logo:** Icon centered above "GajuFreight".
* **Standalone Icon (App Icon / Favicon):** Square-cropped 1:1 format suitable for web browser tabs, mobile apps, and UI badges.
* **Monochrome / Single-Color Versions:** Solid black and solid white for stamps, shipping labels, and single-color print media.

---

## 6. Summary Checklist for the Designer
- [ ] Matches the sleek, geometric style of GajuMarket and QPQ products.
- [ ] Incorporates subtle logistics/freight themes (movement, shipping, connectivity).
- [ ] Delivered as a fully vector-compliant **SVG** file.
- [ ] Scalable and legibly tested at micro-sizes (16x16 favicon) up to large shipping container decals.
